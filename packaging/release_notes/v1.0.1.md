## Fixes
- **Characters' reflections in the water** (Enhanced world > Water reflections): your character, villagers and other characters now reflect as whole figures seen from below. Before, the reflection was pieced together from what the camera sees, so the face floated away from the head, a patch of hair sat below it, and the body almost vanished in shallow water near the shore. This works with the Mii mask on or off, on every region's disc. Characters are drawn a second time, mirrored under the water; the rest of the world reflects as before. The cost is about 0.6 ms per frame at 4K, and nothing changes with water reflections off.
- **Black corners in the sky on ultrawide screens:** at 21:9 and wider, the town's sky had black rectangles in the top corners above the horizon. The sky and clouds now reach the edges of any aspect ratio. 16:9 is unchanged. (At night the stars still only appear in the middle 16:9 part of the sky.)
