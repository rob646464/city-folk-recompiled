## Fixes (Enhanced world > Water reflections)
- **Your head no longer goes missing from the reflection.** Standing a few steps back from a cliff edge or a bank above a pond, the reflection showed the body without the head. The head and body now always reflect together, and a cliff in front of the water hides the reflection's feet first, as it should.
- **The Mii mask's face reflects.** The reflection showed the Mii head with a blank face; the eyes, eyebrows, mouth and glasses now show too, also when seen from a cliff top.
- **Bridges reflect as whole bridges.** The mirrored bridge used to be a ragged fragment pieced together from what the camera sees. The bridge is now drawn mirrored under the river, with the full arch and a clean outline, and hides the reflection of a player standing on it.
- **Fainter over the shallows.** On the beach the reflection started at full strength on the sand-coloured surf. It is now faint over shallow water and grows stronger toward the deep blue water, with no visible line. Reflections over ponds and rivers are more visible than in 1.0.1.
- **Cleaner edges:** the reflections of cliffs and banks along the waterline end in a soft edge instead of ragged pieces.

Water reflections cost about 0.3 ms more per frame at 4K than in 1.0.1. Nothing changes with water reflections off.
