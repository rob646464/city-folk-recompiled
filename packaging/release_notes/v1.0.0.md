**Animal Crossing: City Folk Recompiled 1.0.0** is the first public release of a native PC port of Animal Crossing: City Folk (Wii; *Let's Go to the City* in Europe and Australia, *Machi e Ikou yo: Doubutsu no Mori* in Japan). The game's code is translated from its PowerPC machine code into C++ and compiled for your computer, so it runs natively on Windows, Linux and macOS with no emulator. It is built like our Luigi's Mansion port, on the Aurora engine (WebGPU/Dawn + SDL3).

**No game data is included.** On first start the game asks for your own City Folk disc: a Wii disc image (ISO, WBFS, RVZ, WIA, CISO, GCZ) or a Dolphin "Extract Entire Disc" folder. Supported: **revision 1** of the USA (RUUE01), European (RUUP01), Japanese (RUUJ01) and Korean (RUUK01) releases. Setup identifies the disc by its game code, refuses revision 0 and modified game code, and warns when other game files differ from the retail disc (a damaged or modified dump). One disc of any region is a complete install, since every disc already carries every region's events and items. Setup then builds the game for your PC with the bundled compiler, which takes about a minute. Read the README.txt in the package for details.

## Features
Every option is in the **F1** menu (Esc closes it). Everything that changes the game is off by default, so the game plays as it did on the Wii until you turn it on.

**Graphics**
- **Any resolution, widescreen and ultrawide.** The game's own 16:9 mode at any window size, wider screens in Hor+, and a render-resolution slider. The HUD can stay at 16:9 or spread to the screen's edges. Multisample anti-aliasing is also available.
- **High frame rates.** The game logic runs at its original rate (60 updates per second, 50 for 50 Hz European games), and the frames in between are interpolated, up to 320 fps. "Match display refresh rate" and VRR/G-SYNC aware pacing are included, and "Original" keeps the console's frame rate.
- **Accurate Wii rendering,** checked against Dolphin place by place: EFB copies (including to RAM, so photos and in-game captures work), depth textures, fog, TEV/indirect effects and copy filters. A shader precache prepares the pipelines before the game starts.
- **Enhanced lighting** (optional; Low / Medium / High / Ultra presets): sun and moon shadows from the game clock, soft shadows, up to 256 coloured point lights (lit windows, lamps in the city, fireflies, fireworks), furniture lights in houses and shops (lamps, TVs, fireplaces, candles), ambient occlusion, bloom, time-of-day grading and sun shafts.
- **Enhanced world** (optional): water reflections, wet ground and puddles in the rain, snow sparkle, haze on grey days, and seasonal particles (cherry petals, autumn leaves, snowflakes).
- **CRT filter** with RGB, S-Video, composite and RF signal looks.
- **Texture packs** in Dolphin's format, with a texture dumper for pack makers.

**Controls**
- **Wii pointer from the mouse, a stick or gyro,** and a default gamepad layout for Xbox, PlayStation, Switch and other controllers (based on the Wii Classic Controller layout). Bindings, dead zones, sensitivity and rumble can all be changed per port.
- **Menus with a normal controller:** "Snap pointer to menu items" moves the pointer from item to item with the D-pad or stick, and puts it on the highlighted choice when a choice box appears. The pointer jumps to the choice wherever it was on screen (unless you are moving the mouse), and two-panel screens such as the recycle bin and letters let the D-pad move between the panel and your pockets.
- **Real Wii Remotes** over Bluetooth or a Mayflash DolphinBar (mode 4): buttons, IR pointer (needs a sensor bar), Nunchuk, rumble and the Remote speaker.
- **Type with your PC keyboard** wherever the game takes a USB keyboard (letters, chat, names, designs).
- DSU (cemuhook) motion controllers.

**Online**
- **Visit other players' towns again** (F1 > Online). The lobby lists open rooms, so you can join with one click, with an optional password, Friends only rooms, 2 to 4 players, and LAN rooms in the same list. Connections are encrypted and direct between players, with hole punching and a relay when two routers cannot reach each other. The lobby only introduces players to each other and never sees game data.
- **Wii Speak voice chat** from your PC microphone (off by default; no recording device is opened until you turn it on) and **keyboard chat** during visits.
- **Letters between towns** during visits, and optionally through your own email account ("Always online mail", Windows only).
- **Nintendo events** (off by default): the letters with items and the designs Nintendo sent City Folk players over WiiConnect24 from 2008 to 2012, delivered through the game's own WiiConnect24 code (Pete brings the letters). You can get them in turn, on their original dates, or when you choose. "Receive now" gives each event once per town. The archive is downloaded at run time from WiiLink and is never part of the package.

**Gameplay options**
- **Content from other regions:** every region's events and holidays in your town, plus choices for the Autumn Moon, Mother's and Father's Day dates, the title screen logo and villager house styles. All of it is on your disc already.
- **Hemisphere:** Northern (original) or Southern, with the seasons shifted by six months.
- **Real-time weather** (off by default): pick your town or city by name, and the game's weather follows the real weather there (from Open-Meteo; only the place's rounded coordinates are sent).
- **Grass wear:** Original, New Leaf style or Off.
- **Skin tone** for the resident you are playing.
- **Unused items at Nook's** (Unused content tab): 19 items that are in the game but were never sold, offered one at a time.

**Your saves and Miis**
- **Import a save** from Dolphin, a Wii NAND backup or the SD card's data.bin (with your own console's SD key), and export the port's save back to Dolphin (F1 > Game > Save data). Every import is checked with the game's own checksums and the old save is backed up first.
- **Mii maker** (F1 > Miis): there is no Mii Channel, so the port has its own Mii maker with every Wii Mii option, for Harriet's Mii mask makeover at Shampoodle. Miis can also be imported and exported.
- **Move in from Wild World:** Rover's "Move from the DS!" works with a Wild World save file in place of a DS.
- **Photos to a folder:** in the game's Photos window, SD Card > Save Photo writes the photo (the game's own 896x480 JPEG, `RUU_0001.JPG`, ...) into a `screenshots` folder next to the game. Erase All never deletes these files. F1 > Game > SD Card has the switch and "Open screenshots folder".

**The program**
- First-run setup for your own disc. Several regions can be installed side by side; pick which one to play in F1 > Game.
- **Automatic updates:** the game checks GitHub for new releases at startup, in the background (F1 > Game > Updates; it can be turned off). Downloads are checked against their SHA-256 before anything is replaced, and settings and saves are never touched.
- **Portable mode:** put a file named `portable.txt` next to the program to keep everything in a `portable` folder next to it.

## System requirements
- **Windows:** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, a graphics card with Direct3D 12.
- **Linux:** x86-64, glibc 2.34 or newer (Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Fedora 36+, SteamOS 3.5+), a Vulkan driver, X11 or Wayland.
- **macOS:** macOS 13 or newer on Apple silicon (M1 or newer).
- About 2 GB of free disk space for the game module and shader caches.
- Enhanced lighting at Ultra, 4K and high frame rates need a strong graphics card. The default settings run on modest hardware.

## Installing
1. Unpack the package anywhere (on Windows, not inside Program Files).
2. Start `CityFolkRecomp.exe` (Linux: `CityFolkRecompiled.sh`, macOS: `CityFolkRecomp`).
3. On the setup screen, pick your disc and click "Install". The game code is translated and compiled for your PC (about a minute), then the shaders are prepared.

Settings and saves live in `%APPDATA%\Animal Crossing City Folk Recompiled` (Linux: `~/.config`, `~/.local/share` and `~/.cache`; macOS: `~/Library/Application Support`). When reporting a problem, attach `last_run.log` from that folder.

## Known issues
- **macOS** is built by GitHub Actions and has not been run by the developers. It is not signed or notarized (see the note above).
- **Online play** has been tested between two PCs on one network and through the relay. Visits between homes behind every kind of router have had little testing so far. On Linux and macOS, "Always online mail" is not available yet.
- The first time a new kind of effect or place appears, a short stutter can occur while its shaders compile. The shader cache keeps them for later sessions.
- At some interpolated frames, a faint dotted seam can show on characters' faces.
- Enhanced lighting and Enhanced world are new; their look may still change in updates.
