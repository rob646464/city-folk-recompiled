Data services the program can use (both optional, off by default):
- Real-time weather: weather data by Open-Meteo.com (https://open-meteo.com), CC BY 4.0.
- Nintendo events: distribution archive courtesy of WiiLink (https://wiilink.ca), downloaded
  at run time, never included in the package.

No Nintendo code or data is included: the game is built on your PC from your own disc.
